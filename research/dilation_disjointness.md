# Multiplicative dilation correlations of the centre

Attack on prize problem 2 via the Kátai–Bourgain–Sarnak–Ziegler criterion,
as specified in [_astra_ideas5.md](_astra_ideas5.md) item 5. Exact
correlations \(C_{p,q}(N)\) for primes through 13 were measured. The
symbolic pass on the pair \((2,3)\) produced only independently growing
cones. There is no estimate stronger than \(|C|\le N\). This is not a
prize claim, and random-looking ratios are not used as evidence.

Certifier: `research/dilation_disjointness.py`. Dump:
`research/dilation_disjointness.json`. Does not modify `experiment.py`,
`strip_graph.py`, or `strip_extend.py`.

## Target

Put \(s_n=(-1)^{c_n}\) and

\[
C_{p,q}(N)=\sum_{n=1}^{N}s_{pn}s_{qn},\qquad p\neq q\text{ prime}.
\]

If \(C_{p,q}(N)=o(N)\) for every distinct prime pair, the
Bourgain–Sarnak–Ziegler criterion with multiplicative test function \(1\)
would give \(\sum_{n\le N}s_n=o(N)\), which is equivalent to density
\(1/2\). The missing step is a deterministic estimate, not a plot.

The joint mechanism under test is the pair of powers \(F^2\) and \(F^3\)
acting on the same seed row. Left-permutivity of each power (checked on
the 5-cell and 7-cell truth tables) pairs generic configurations; it does
not pair times on this orbit. Both extreme seed bits outside the triangle
are frozen at 0.

## Measured correlations

Centre bits through time \(13\cdot 2^{14}\) were generated by the packed
update and checked against `experiment.center_bits` on a prefix of length
256. Horizons \(N=2^8,2^{10},2^{12},2^{14}\). At \(N=2^{14}\),

\[
\frac{C_{2,3}(N)}{N}=0.01196,\qquad
\frac{D(N)}{N}=-0.01025,
\]

and every other prime pair through 13 has \(|C_{p,q}(N)|/N\le 0.0152\).
These numbers are recorded; they are not an \(o(N)\) theorem.

## Identities that fail

On the product sequence \(\pi_n=c_{2n}\oplus c_{3n}\) of length \(2^{14}\):

- linear complexity 8191 (Berlekamp–Massey),
- 8094 ones, signed mean \(0.01196\),
- all four pairs \((c_{2n},c_{3n})\) occur (counts 1023, 1018, 1012, 1043
  on a length-4096 prefix),
- 2038 runs, longest run 10.

None of the preregistered GF(2) relations holds on \(n=1..4096\):
\(c_{2n}+c_{3n}\), that sum plus \(1\), plus \(c_n\), plus \(c_{6n}\),
plus \(c_{2n+1}\), plus \(c_{3n+1}\), plus \(c_{n+1}\), the two-term
relations with \(c_n\), or \(c_{2n}+c_{3n}+c_n+c_{6n}\).

None of the short coboundaries equals \(s_{2n}s_{3n}\) identically:
\(s_n s_{n+1}\), \(s_{2n}s_{2n+2}\), \(s_{3n}s_{3n+3}\), \(s_n s_{2n}\),
\(s_n s_{3n}\), \(s_{2n+1}s_{3n+1}\).

## Joint cones for \((F^2,F^3)\)

\(F^2\) is a 5-cell map, \(F^3\) a 7-cell map. Closed windows that iterate
the two centres grow at speeds 4 and 6:

\[
|W_k(F^2)|=4k+1,\qquad |W_k(F^3)|=6k+1.
\]

Along the orbit, the 5-cell window at time \(2n\) does not determine the
7-cell window at time \(3n\) (4096 samples: 32 distinct 5-windows, 128
distinct 7-windows, up to 91 sevens per five). The converse fails as well.
The product \(s_{2n}s_{3n}\) *is* a function of the pair of windows,
because those windows contain the two centres. That is tautological.

Interior half-widths 0, 1, 2, 3 around both centres likewise determine
the product, again because the centres sit inside the key.

Bounded *edge* histories do not. For every tested width
\(W\in\{1,2,3,4,6,8,12,16\}\), the fibre of
\((L_{2n},L_{3n},R_{2n},R_{3n})\) of width \(W\) takes both product
signs on some (usually every) key. Left edges alone are mixed as well;
on this seed they are the constant bit 1, so a single left-edge key
already sees both signs.

## Coupling \(w_n=F^n(z_n)\)

Write \(z_n\) for the row at time \(2n\) and \(w_n\) for the row at time
\(3n\). Exact coupling is \(w_n=F^n(z_n)\), whose light cone on \(z_n\)
has half-width \(n\) and grows. A *bounded* centred window of \(z_n\)
does not determine \(c_{3n}\) on this orbit for half-widths
\(0,1,2,4,8\): every such window class that is large enough to repeat
takes both values of \(c_{3n}\), and the product \(s_{2n}s_{3n}\) is
mixed.

Half-widths 16, 32, and 64 appear to determine \(c_{3n}\) on the
\(N_{\mathrm{sym}}=2^{12}\) sample, but every key is unique
(\(n_{\mathrm{windows}}=n_{\mathrm{count}}\)). That is a finite-sample
artifact, not a bounded-range function. It is not used as a coupling.

## Why it died

Preregistered kill: the symbolic pass produces only two independently
growing cones, with no relation enabling an estimate stronger than the
trivial bound. Random-looking correlation plots count as failure.

The only seed-valid couplings found are the growing cone
\(w_n=F^n(z_n)\) and tautological interior windows that already contain
both centres. Bounded edge histories do not determine \(s_{2n}s_{3n}\).
Closed windows grow at speeds 4 and 6. No estimate stronger than
\(|C|\le N\) follows, so BSZ is not applied.

This is not a proof that the dilations fail to be disjoint, and it is
not a prize claim.
