1. **Non-eventual-constancy of \(b_k=c_{2^k}\) via AND-injections — Problem 1**

   Packed Rule 30 is Rule 150 XOR adjacent ANDs, and Rule 150 keeps the
   centre at 1 by palindrome. So \(b_k=0\) iff the Green’s function of
   those injections, evaluated at time \(2^k\), has odd parity.
   Survive only with a proof that this parity is not eventually
   constant (equivalently, that \((b_k)\) is not eventually constant).
   That gives \(v_k\ne v_{k+1}\) for every \(k\); if \((b_k)\) is not
   eventually periodic, the 2-kernel is infinite and \(c\) is
   aperiodic. Cycle W already killed Boolean templates for \(b_k\).
